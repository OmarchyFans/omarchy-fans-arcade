# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Brick Blitz, an original brick-breaker, plays straight from the bar with nothing else to install.
- Five Brick Blitz layouts that loop faster, tough bricks, an extra ball per level and a saved high score.
- Pac-Man, Galaga and Super Street Fighter II launch in MAME from your own ROM files.
- Arcade checks your ROMs with MAME first and tells you exactly which file is missing or wrong.
- The Arcade chip tells you when a new version is published and shows what changed.
